# AutoTap
a free python tapper for tiktok lives (or whatever you want to use it for)

## Controls & Hotkeys
* **F8:** Toggle key auto-presser ON / OFF (defaults to `L` at 5 presses/sec)
* **F9:** Toggle left mouse auto-clicker ON / OFF (defaults to 10 clicks/sec)
* **Esc:** Immediately stop and exit the app

## How to Use for TikTok Live on PC
1. Open **Auto Tap** and adjust your key or click rates if desired.
2. Click inside your **TikTok** browser tab or desktop window to make sure it is the **active window**.
3. Press **F8** to start auto-pressing `L` to send likes.
4. Hover your cursor over the **heart icon** on the stream and press **F9** to combine auto-clicking with `L` presses for **dual (high-speed) liking**.
5. Press **F8** / **F9** again to pause, or press **Esc** anywhere to close the program.
